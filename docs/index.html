What stood out

Three games carried the average

Indiana (Jul 9), Portland (Aug 16) and Connecticut (Jul 19) pulled the average above the median, so the median is the better guide to a typical night.

Star power is hard to read

Aug 16 drew 16,513 though Kelsey Plum sat out injured. Her only home appearance, Aug 25, drew 10,459. Aug 16 was a Sunday, which may matter more.

Four figures repeat

Three June games all list 9,234, and Jul 17 matches a road crowd in Las Vegas. All four match WNBA.com box scores, so they stay in, flagged.
